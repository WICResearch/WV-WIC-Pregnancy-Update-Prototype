Pregnancy Information Update

Update your pregnancy information

We understand that pregnancy circumstances can change. If your pregnancy has ended, you can use this form to privately let WIC know without having to explain what happened over the phone.

You do not need to tell us the details of your loss.

We will only ask for information needed to identify your WIC record and determine what happens next with your WIC services.

You may still choose to speak with your WIC clinic at any time.

Prototype notice: This demonstration does not send information to WV WIC, Crossroads, or any other system. Please do not enter real participant information.
